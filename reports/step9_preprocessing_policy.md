### Core Preprocessing Principles
* **Immutability of Raw Data:** `data/complete soil data.xlsx` remains strictly read-only and unaltered.
* **Reproducibility:** All cleaning, transformation, and imputation will be implemented as modular, parameter-free or cross-validation-contained Python code in Step 10.
* **Leakage-Free Architecture:** Any fit-transform operations (imputation medians, standard scalers, label encoders) must be computed exclusively on training folds during cross-validation.
* **No Blind Deletions:** Statistical outliers representing real agronomic conditions and rare minority crops are preserved to ensure practical field utility.

### 3.3 Component 3: Missing Humidity Handling
* **Primary Policy:** Wrap `SimpleImputer(strategy='median')` within an `sklearn.pipeline.Pipeline`.
* **Execution:** For the 20 Mydukur rows (S.NO 267–286), humidity is imputed to **`72.59%`** (training fold median).
* **Sensitivity Flag:** Maintain an optional command-line flag `--humidity-imputer=mice` allowing comparative evaluation against the $64.11\%$ weather-conditioned regression value.

### 3.4 Component 4: Target Variable Canonical Mapping
* **Primary Policy:** Apply lookup dictionary from `data/processed/crop_label_mapping.csv`.
* **Standardization:** Map raw labels into 34 canonical classes.
* **Supervised Experiment Filter ($N \ge 4$):**
  * Crops retained: `Paddy` (143), `Black Gram` (95), `Cotton` (93), `Groundnut` (61), `Turmeric` (35), `Bajra` (34), `Bengal Gram` (30), `Sweet Orange` (17), `Banana` (14), `Sunflower` (13), `Soybean` (9), `Onion` (8), `Chamanthi` (8 with Mums), `Jowar` (7), `Chilli` (7 with Red/Green), `Vegetables` (6), `Sesame` (6), `Tomato` (4), `Korra` (4).
  * Total samples: $601$ out of $611$ ($98.36\%$ data retention).
  * Enables clean, uncompromised 5-fold Stratified Cross-Validation.
